Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

1. Scope and contact

This notice describes the data-handling arrangements for Ramzy's Slide Architect, an independently published, free ChatGPT plugin operated by Ahmed Ramzy, an individual based in Saudi Arabia. For questions or requests about personal information that the Publisher directly holds, email arramdan@gmail.com. The tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI.

2. Two separate processing contexts

Content inside ChatGPT: The plugin structures prompts, uploaded files, tables, and document content within the ChatGPT platform. The current plugin has no Publisher-operated external backend, analytics service, database, or API receiving copies of uploaded content. The Publisher does not receive ordinary ChatGPT user uploads through a separate plugin server. OpenAI's own processing, retention, account controls, and privacy practices are governed by OpenAI's Privacy Policy and the terms/settings that apply to the user's account. The Publisher cannot access, delete, or guarantee the retention period of content held by OpenAI merely because the user employed the plugin.

Direct communications and website visits: If you voluntarily email support, the Publisher receives your email address, message and any information you choose to provide. The informational website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may process technical access data under its own policies. These channels are separate from the ChatGPT plugin's content-processing workflow.

3. Categories of information and purposes

Within ChatGPT, materials may include user prompts, uploaded text, documents, tabular data, optional identifiers included in those files, and resulting blueprints, for the purpose of preparing the output you requested. The Publisher does not independently collect those materials through its own infrastructure.

For direct support, the Publisher may process your contact email, correspondence, problem description, and any attachments you intentionally send, solely to respond, troubleshoot, manage a related follow-up, and address legitimate legal obligations. Please do not include confidential source files, passwords, API tokens, or unnecessary sensitive personal data in support messages.

ChatGPT processing is provided by OpenAI under its applicable notices; see its information about service providers and international processing. Support messages are received through the email service associated with the published Gmail address (Google), which may process email metadata and content according to its own terms. GitHub hosts this public website. The Publisher does not sell user-submitted content or operate an advertising profile built from uploaded presentation files. The Publisher does not intentionally forward support correspondence to additional marketing or analytics providers. Any legally required disclosures remain subject to applicable law.

ChatGPT content: Retention and deletion follow OpenAI's applicable policies and the controls available in your ChatGPT account; the Publisher does not set an independent retention period for that platform content.

Support correspondence: Support email is kept only as needed to handle the inquiry, related follow-up, or legal obligations. Support correspondence is ordinarily retained for no longer than 12 months after the last substantive communication relating to the support request, after which the Publisher will delete it, unless continued retention is required or permitted by applicable law, necessary for an unresolved dispute, or otherwise justified by a lawful exception. The Publisher has adopted this retention policy for the support mailbox. You may request deletion of support correspondence when feasible by emailing the support address.

Website: Any infrastructure logs retained by GitHub are governed by GitHub's own practices rather than a Publisher-controlled analytics database.
