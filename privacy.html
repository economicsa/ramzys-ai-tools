Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. About this tool

Ramzy's Slide Architect is an independent, ChatGPT-based workflow that helps users organize content they provide into structured presentation or report blueprints. It does not itself create the finished PowerPoint file or independently verify the factual accuracy of source materials.

2. Content processed within ChatGPT

When you use the tool, the ChatGPT platform may process your prompts, uploaded documents, text, tables, and generated blueprint content to perform your requested task. The tool currently uses instructions and supporting reference files within ChatGPT. It does not require a separate publisher-operated server, database, or API to process your source documents.

The publisher does not receive a separate copy of the content you submit to ChatGPT through the tool's own backend, because no such backend is operated for this version. This statement does not describe or override OpenAI's handling of information on its platform.

3. Platform data handling

Data processed through ChatGPT is subject to the platform's applicable privacy notices, terms, retention practices, and user controls. Please refer to OpenAI's Privacy Policy and the settings available in your ChatGPT account. We cannot control or promise deletion of information retained by the platform.

4. Additional services and sharing

This version of Ramzy's Slide Architect does not send uploaded source files to an additional external processing service operated or selected by the publisher, and it does not require a connection to a publisher-controlled external data store. If the workflow changes to use such services, this policy should be updated before that functionality is made available.

5. Support messages

If you voluntarily contact support at arramdan@gmail.com, the publisher will receive your email address and the content of your message. These communications are handled through the email service used for that address, separately from ChatGPT. Please do not send passwords, API keys, sensitive personal information, or confidential source documents in support requests.

For questions about a support email you sent, including a request to remove correspondence held by the publisher where feasible, contact the same address. Email handling may also be subject to the email provider's policies and applicable obligations.

6. Website hosting

This informational website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with visits under its own applicable privacy policy. The website is separate from the content-processing workflow in ChatGPT.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be revised if the tool's features or processing arrangements change. The date above indicates the most recent published revision. For privacy questions, contact arramdan@gmail.com.
